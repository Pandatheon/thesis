\chapter{Acknowledgments}
\label{Chapter: Acknowledgments}
    After a two-year journey, my Master's studies are coming to an end.
    When the international flight landed in Germany,
    I looked out of the window at Frankfurt Airport in the early hours of the morning
    and realized that this was the first time I had left China for another country.
    But back then, I could not imagine how much I would gain during my studies.
    Much of it came from the one and a half years I spent at SYSCOP.
    In retrospect, there are many people I would like to thank.

    First, I would like to thank Prof.~Dr.~Moritz Diehl. 
    When I first emailed him about a possible HiWi position,
    he replied quickly and warmly offered me opportunities.
    This essentially made my stay at SYSCOP possible, and with it this Master's thesis.
    I also greatly appreciated the open and friendly atmosphere he has built in the lab 
    as well as all the events he has organized,
    which made SYSCOP not only an inspiring place to work but also a welcoming place to belong.

    When I started as a student assistant, I was not yet very skilled at the work.
    My supervisor, Jonathan Frey, spared no effort in helping me.
    He patiently helped me get started, showed me how to contribute properly to the repository,
    and shared a lot of valuable coding experience.
    All this fostered both my implementation skills and my interest in numerical solvers and optimization.
    He later also supervised this thesis and guided me through it with many good suggestions.
    My other supervisor, Katrin Baumgärtner, was also a great help to me.
    She, together with Jonathan, provided many interesting ideas for shaping this thesis,
    and her advice greatly improved its quality.
    I am deeply grateful to them both.

    I also want to thank all my colleagues and friends at SYSCOP
    for the Mensa trains and coffee chats that made every day an interesting one.
    It has been a privilege to be surrounded by
    such a group of smart and kind people with enthusiasm for optimization and control.
    I look up to many of them as my role models in research.

    Thanks also to all my friends, with whom I play cards, go hiking, and travel to different countries.
    They made life here not just about study and work, but also about fun and adventure.

    Finally, I want to thank my parents, who unconditionally supported me in studying in Germany
    and always told me to treat myself well with the money they sent,
    to which I always replied that I have already treated myself well enough.
